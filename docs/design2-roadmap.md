# Design 2 roadmap

Requested order, 2026-09-28, corrected by the user on 2026-09-29. Poker and
Tarot are separate decks with their own dimensions and matching backs.
Minchiate describes the Tarot deck's 97-card inventory and tradition; it is
not a separate deck choice or a subset of Poker.

1. Compose the Tarot deck of Design 2 at 825 × 1425 (2.75 × 4.75 in), matching
   the existing Tarot back and silhouette. The earlier 750 × 1050 exports were
   incorrectly made at Poker size. All 97 faces are now recomposed from
   preserved illustration components for the tall frame and are in layout
   review; the old exports remain archived.
   Use the 97-card Minchiate inventory, confirmed by the user: 56 suited cards,
   40 trumps and the Fool, following Design 1's current inventory.
   Preserve selected masters and exact prompts alongside the existing sources.
2. Balance the Tarot deck against its own frame and back, and review the
   separate Design 2 Poker deck against its own frame and back.
   Enforce the common center and consistent borders across faces and backs;
   review the interiors in that geometry. Existing registration reports cover
   the current cards, but do not establish acceptance of the future expanded set.
   Use the single-card viewer and rotation to review transitions and balance,
   then verify dimensions, alpha, border geometry and center placement.
3. Expand the approved French-suited design into Bridge, Travel and Jumbo
   formats. Tarot retains its own deck and size; do not make tarot faces for
   each French-suited format. Draw and compose artwork for each format rather
   than resizing flattened Poker cards. Review each format with its shared
   center and consistent borders.
4. Derive European Standard from the finished Bridge set by resizing. This is
   the only resizing exception, because those formats are nearly the same size.
5. Refresh inventories, catalogs, generated viewer pages and audit records after
   each approved promotion; package the expanded design once its sets are ready.

Current inventory: 54 Poker faces, 97 Tarot faces in layout review, 30 backs
across six sizes, and 12 blank face frames. The
[Tarot construction notes](../designs/design2/docs/design/tarot-faces-v1.md)
record the four-panel frame, traditional Chinese names and five initial
costume revisions. All 97 Tarot compositions share the native center and
frame; earlier Poker-sized exports are retained byte-for-byte in sources.

Before format expansion, address the
[Chinese visual-direction review](../designs/design2/docs/design/chinese-visual-direction.md)
requested on 2026-09-29. Prioritize Leo, Air and architectural settings, then
check ships, costume and objects against specific references. The target
audience is Chinese viewers; the completed layout is not cultural validation.
The user subsequently confirmed that Chinese visual traditions take priority
over European pictorial attributes while names, ranks and usability remain.
The first revision group covers Faith, Leo, Air, Water, House of the Devil,
World and Trumpets. Further costume, object and allegory review remains part
of this stage before format expansion.

The next stage is reviewing the native Tarot composition and the
[Chinese name panels](../designs/design2/docs/design/chinese-name-panels.md),
including Chinese-language editorial review of the working translations.
Use the [costume and textile guide](../designs/design2/docs/design/costume-and-textile-direction.md)
to settle garment families and motif placement alongside those layout proofs.
Review Tarot with its matching back, and review the separate French-suited
Poker faces with their matching backs before format expansion.
Current Tarot center measurements use painted visual mass;
the French courts and backs use their central decorative landmarks. Their
reports document those different measurements, without establishing that all
interiors have received a final visual approval.
