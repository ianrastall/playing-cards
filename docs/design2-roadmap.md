# Design 2 roadmap

Requested order, 2026-09-28. The Design 1 release and collection viewer are the
immediate work; the stages below are subsequent artwork work.

1. Create the tarot cards for the Poker set of Design 2 (750 × 1050).
   Use the 97-card Minchiate inventory, confirmed by the user: 56 suited cards,
   40 trumps and the Fool, following Design 1's current inventory.
   Preserve selected masters and exact prompts alongside the existing sources.
2. Balance the complete Design 2 Poker set, including the new tarot faces.
   Enforce the common center and consistent borders across faces and backs;
   review the interiors in that geometry. Existing registration reports cover
   the current cards, but do not establish acceptance of the future expanded set.
   Use the single-card viewer and rotation to review transitions and balance,
   then verify dimensions, alpha, border geometry and center placement.
3. Expand the approved design into Bridge, Travel, Jumbo and Tarot formats.
   Draw and compose artwork for each format rather than resizing flattened Poker
   cards. Review each format with its shared center and consistent borders.
4. Derive European Standard from the finished Bridge set by resizing. This is
   the only resizing exception, because those formats are nearly the same size.
5. Refresh inventories, catalogs, generated viewer pages and audit records after
   each approved promotion; package the expanded design once its sets are ready.

Current inventory: 54 finished Poker faces, 30 backs across six sizes, and 12
blank face frames. No new Design 2 tarot artwork has been generated in this task.
