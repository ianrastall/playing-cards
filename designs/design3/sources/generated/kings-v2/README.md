# Rugged Celtic Poker Kings — v2

Four approved source masters generated with built-in ImageGen. The user requested
the four Kings only, with a rugged, nature-oriented Celtic treatment and the
familiar court attributes. No Queens or Jacks were generated in this revision.

| Suit | Selected master | Pixels | Upper portrait and attribute |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spades | [king-spades-v2.png](king-spades-v2.png) | 1061 × 1483 | Slight right turn, both eyes; upright sword |
| Hearts | [king-hearts-v2.png](king-hearts-v2.png) | 1060 × 1484 | Both eyes, no moustache; sword behind head |
| Diamonds | [king-diamonds-v2.png](king-diamonds-v2.png) | 1060 × 1484 | Strict left profile, one eye; axe |
| Clubs | [king-clubs-v2.png](king-clubs-v2.png) | 1060 × 1484 | Left three-quarter, both eyes; sword and orb |

All selected masters are unchanged copies of the tool outputs. The
[manifest](manifest.json) records hashes, actual dimensions, visual pose checks
and exact half-turn comparisons. The Spades output is close to 5:7 but differs
from the requested dimensions; the other three have exact 5:7 proportions.
No resizing, cropping, painting or alpha changes were applied.

[prompts.json](prompts.json) preserves four generation prompts and two focused
Hearts repair prompts. The first Hearts output retained a moustache and a wrong
lower sword. The first repair corrected the face and blade but introduced an
extra lower hand. The second repair removed that extra fist/guard and restored
a coherent sleeve. Both intermediate files remain here for provenance and are
excluded from the active gallery.

The original Celtic King was the Spades composition/frame reference. The new
Spades was the companion Kings' style reference; Design 1 Hearts, Diamonds and
Clubs supplied pose/attribute references only. Museum illustrations were not
used as raster inputs.

Coarse green wool, oatmeal linen, red lining, weathered faces, oak branches and
acorns replace the earlier polished court figures. Circlets, torcs and brooches
keep royal status readable. The Clubs orb's spiral finial adapts its traditional
attribute to this Celtic direction; it is a fictional design choice.

The user approved these four paintings and requested official, balanced cards.
These source files remain unchanged and do not have exact rotational symmetry.
The separate [official faces](../../../cards/faces/french-suited/poker/) now have
a shared Celtic frame and indices, a centered half-turn-safe spiral medallion,
exact opposing portraits and rounded 750 × 1050 production geometry.
See the [registration record](../../../docs/design/kings-registration-v1.md).
