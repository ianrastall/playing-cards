# Celtic Poker Jacks and Jokers — first review set

Four Jacks and two Jokers extend the rugged Kings and accepted Queen direction.
Built-in ImageGen generated all six paintings using the Celtic Spades Queen
as the style reference. Design 1 cards supplied pose and composition references
only. Coarse green wool, plain linen, narrow woven trim, bronze ornaments,
oak leaves and acorns continue the same fictional Celtic artistic synthesis.

| Card | Upper portrait | Attribute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jack of Spades | Left profile; one eye; small moustache | Upright infantry spear |
| Jack of Hearts | Right profile; one eye; clean jaw | Raised long green leaf |
| Jack of Diamonds | Left three-quarter; both eyes | Slender straight sword |
| Jack of Clubs | Right three-quarter; both eyes | Pointed shaft with cream fletching |
| Black Joker | Smiling bearded male storyteller; both eyes | Carved fool's staff and open storytelling gesture |
| Red Joker | Smiling woman bard; both eyes | Small triangular wooden harp |

Young adult faces and simple soft cloth caps distinguish the Jacks from the
Kings and Queens. The Jokers have modest cloth caps with bronze bells, lively
expressions and distinct props. The harp, fool's staff and entertainer clothing
are artistic cues, not claims to reconstruct a particular historical performer.
The Jokers use black or red stacked JOKER indices without suit pips.

The [Jack masters](../../sources/generated/jacks-v1/README.md) and
[Joker masters](../../sources/generated/jokers-v1/README.md) remain unchanged,
with exact prompts, SHA-256 hashes and original dimensions. Balanced review PNGs
live in [jacks-jokers-registration-v1](../../sources/components/jacks-jokers-registration-v1/).

All six use the approved Kings' unchanged shared frame, field mask, paired side
roundels, central double spiral and 3.5 mm rounded alpha outline. J/suit indices
share the established positions; five spaced serif letters fit each Joker panel.
Each face is 750 × 1050 RGBA at 300 ppi. The exact center is (374.5, 524.5), and
each complete image is pixel-identical after a 180-degree turn. The upper portrait
supplies the lower rotated portrait, with the existing narrow wool blend at the waist.

The [registration manifest](jacks-jokers-registration-v1.json) and
[audit](jacks-jokers-registration-v1-audit.json) record source and component hashes,
border equality, center ornament, symmetry, alpha, DPI and reconstruction checks.
These six faces are for review and remain separate from the production catalog.
