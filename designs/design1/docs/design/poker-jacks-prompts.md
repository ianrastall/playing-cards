# Poker jacks — generation prompts

Artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Spades used the current 750 × 1050 Spades king and queen as references. The other jacks used the generated Spades jack as their reference.

Imagegen returned 1060 × 1484 masters. With the user's explicit approval, `scripts/export_face.py` exported 750 × 1050 PNGs at nominal 300 DPI using Pillow Lanczos resampling. Untouched masters are under `sources/generated/poker/jacks/`; current playable/printable images are under `cards/faces/french-suited/poker/<suit>/jack.png`.

## Spades

INPUT 1: current King of Spades, PRIMARY layout/ornament/palette and male figure rendering reference.
INPUT 2: current Queen of Spades, SECONDARY reference for refined skin/hand rendering and delicate figure scale.
OUTPUT: ONE flat full-card PNG, exact 750 x 1050 pixels, 5:7 portrait poker ratio, 300-DPI if possible. Match input canvas dimensions. Entire card fills canvas, no perspective, shadows, mockup, surrounding scene or multiple cards.

Preserve red-tipped ivory rosettes, olive/gold acanthus leaves, small red berries and pointed palmettes with same fine detail and placements. Maintain shared multicolor palette: navy and madder garments, warm ivory, antique gold, muted olive, near-black floral field, natural skin. NO monochromatic suit color scheme.
REPLACE ROYAL WITH JACK: a dignified young ADULT male court attendant/young nobleman, about 25–30 years old, gracefully athletic rather than bulky, fine naturalistic Art Nouveau face and hands. Same head and upper-body scale as kings/queens, full hat inside interior floral boundary, no enlarged portrait. A tailored high-neck madder court doublet, navy embroidered shoulder mantle and flowing sleeves with fine gold botanical bands, same diagonal joined double-ended waist and center rosette. No royal crown, no orb, no queen jewelry, no heavy beard. Use a soft navy Renaissance cap with narrow gold/madder band, a small rosette fastening and short ivory plume fully inside frame. Dark chestnut hair in soft ear-length waves, a small neat moustache is acceptable, clean jaw.
ESSENTIAL SPADES POSE: upper portrait in STRICT LEFT-FACING PROFILE: nose points to top-left J corner, exactly ONE visible eye, absolutely no second eye or three-quarter view. Slim straight nose, composed attentive expression. Both upper and lower depictions same man in rotated profile.
ATTRIBUTE: replace king's broadsword with a slender decorated infantry PIKE held upright in upper figure's hand on viewer's right, simple wood shaft, small elegant leaf-shaped STEEL spearhead, restrained gold socket, contained below top floral border. It must read as a pike, not a king's broad sword or queen's scepter. Free hand lightly holds mantle near chest, no extra objects.
INDICES: replace K with bold clear serif BLACK 'J' above solid BLACK SPADE, in same upper-left ivory cartouche; exact rotated J/spade at bottom-right. J has a strong curved lower hook and same cap-height/weight as K and Q, not an I or numeral 1. Only two J letters, no names, no extra text.
DOUBLE-ENDED: lower half a precise 180-degree rotated counterpart of upper half, same face, hat, weapon and hands, elegant continuous diagonal robe join, no unrelated second character. Match frame balance.
FINISH: same fine warm contour, softly modeled natural anatomy and rich printed-illustration colors as existing courts. Mucha-style figure drawing within Morris/Safavid botanical ornament. No cartoon, anime, flat clip art, photorealism, distressed texture or new visual style.
