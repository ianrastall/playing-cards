# Design 2 Jokers

Both selected artworks have been accepted by the user as finished.

- [Black Joker](joker-black.png) — black lotus robe, crimson sash and folding fan.
- [Red Joker](joker-red.png) — red lotus robe, teal sash and pellet drum.

Both are Chinese-inspired fictional entertainers, with soft cloth caps and
warm expressions. They share the courts' dense lotus fields, gold framing,
ivory reserve and turquoise center jewel. Stacked JOKER indices appear in
opposing corners; the portraits form visually double-ended compositions.
The clothing and props are artistic interpretations, not a specific historical
costume reconstruction.

Both selected masters are 1060 × 1484 RGB PNGs, generated with built-in ImageGen.
Exact prompts are in [black](joker-black-prompt.json) and
[red](joker-red-prompt.json); dimensions and hashes are in
[manifest.json](manifest.json). The red initial render is retained as provenance;
the selected `joker-red.png` corrects its canvas proportions to 5:7.

Visual review checked the lettering, props, framing and design consistency.
Pixel-exact rotational symmetry and identical border pixels are not claimed.
These are artwork masters; catalog exports remain a separate workflow.
