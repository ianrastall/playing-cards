# Design 2 structural colorways v1

These four built-in ImageGen edits extend the selected nested-red structural
study to the remaining Design 2 colors. Madder Lake remains in the preceding
`design2-structural-study-v2` folder.

All five colorways share the same intended ground hierarchy: a darkest muted
outer floral border, a slightly brighter narrow inner border, a quieter broad
field, and the richest, deepest ground around the central medallion. Accents are
tuned per colorway rather than mechanically recolored:

- Prussian Blue retains warm olive foliage and uses distinctly greener teal
  medallions.
- Verdigris warms the foliage toward yellow-olive and uses indigo medallions.
- Manganese Violet uses a dusty pigment-like field with deep teal medallions.
- Lamp Black separates the four regions through graphite-to-ink values while
  retaining the warm floral palette.

The exact prompts are in `prompts.txt`. These are visual studies, not registered
production backs; generated geometry is not pixel-exact under a half turn.
