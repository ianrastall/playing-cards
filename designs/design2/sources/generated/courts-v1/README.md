# Design 2 Court Cards

The user accepted all twelve court artworks as finished. The revised King of Spades is
the authoritative reference for the court design.

The [four Queens](../../../docs/design/queens-v1.md) are also generated and saved:
[Spades](queen-spades.png), [Hearts](queen-hearts.png),
[Diamonds](queen-diamonds.png), and [Clubs](queen-clubs.png).

The [four Jacks](../../../docs/design/jacks-v1.md) are generated and saved:
[Spades](jack-spades.png), [Hearts](jack-hearts.png),
[Diamonds](jack-diamonds.png), and [Clubs](jack-clubs.png).
Their blue lotus robes and youthful faces distinguish them from the other ranks.
Exact prompts are saved in `jack-<suit>-prompt.json`; dimensions and source
hashes are in [jacks-manifest.json](jacks-manifest.json).
The [two Jokers](../jokers-v1/README.md) and [four aces](../aces-v1/README.md)
are now generated. Number cards are next.

| Suit | Selected artwork | Conventional identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spades | [King of Spades](king-spades-floral-jian-v3.png) | Both eyes; upright jian; user-approved finished design |
| Hearts | [King of Hearts](king-hearts.png) | Both eyes; no moustache; sword behind head |
| Diamonds | [King of Diamonds](king-diamonds.png) | One-eyed profile; inward-facing axe |
| Clubs | [King of Clubs](king-clubs.png) | Both eyes; jian and cross-topped jade-green orb |

Hearts and Diamonds have Madder Lake outer frames and red indices. Spades and
Clubs have Lamp Black frames and black indices. All keep the black interior
floral field, yellow Ming-inspired dragon robes, shaped ivory reserve, dense
lotus ornament, diagonal join and turquoise jewel from the approved design.

All selected masters are 1060 × 1484 RGB PNGs, preserved as generated. Exact
prompts are in each companion's `king-<suit>-prompt.json`; source hashes and
artwork statuses are in [kings-manifest.json](kings-manifest.json).

Hearts received one local correction to remove the moustache from both faces.
The initial Hearts pass and earlier Spades studies are retained as provenance;
use the selected filenames in the table above. The Clubs cross-topped orb is
a deliberate English-pattern playing-card attribute, not historical Ming regalia.
The Diamonds axe is a stylized card attribute, not an authenticated artifact copy.

Visual review checked the distinguishing poses, suit indices, weapons within
the frame, floral density and matched design vocabulary. These source masters
use visually two-way compositions; pixel-exact rotational equality is not
claimed. No subsequent production transformation was made to the approved
Spades artwork. Finished-card catalog exports are a separate workflow.
