# Design 2 structural study v1

This is a built-in ImageGen redesign study made September 22, 2026 from the
Madder Lake Poker back. The production card was used as a style, motif and
palette reference; the study redraws the composition rather than reusing its
registered plates or palette masks.

The selected result is `madder-lake-toranj-refined.png`. It uses one central
toranj medallion, smaller top and bottom sar-toranj pendants, four corner
quarter-medallions, a connected islimi vine and a continuous border with lotus
corner turns. The refinement removes small filler blossoms and simplifies the
vine for better reading at physical card size.

The two exact built-in prompts are in `prompts.txt`. This is a visual study, not
a production back: it has not been registered to the six output formats or made
pixel-exact under a half turn. Its purpose is to settle the new composition
before production reconstruction.
