# Design 2 structural study v2

This built-in ImageGen edit develops the selected Madder Lake structural study.
It leaves the composition intact and changes two aspects:

- the substantial double gold frame at the inner edge of the floral border is
  repeated at its outer edge, immediately inside the ivory margin;
- four nested areas use distinct values of the same Madder Lake hue.

The red hierarchy is dark and muted at the outer floral band, slightly brighter
and clearer at the narrow inner transition, softly muted at middle lightness in
the broad vine field, and richest and deepest around the teal central medallion.
This alternation frames the card, opens the ground and restores the center as the
strongest focal point without introducing a second red hue.

The selected study is `madder-lake-nested-reds.png`; its exact prompt is in
`prompt.txt`. It remains a visual study rather than a registered production back.
