# Design 2 Tarot source revisions

First Tarot-size costume proofs, started 2026-09-29. These components are
composed independently for the 825 × 1425 Tarot frame, with Chinese names
typeset separately. They do not replace the preserved Poker-sized masters.

`prompts.json` records exact built-in ImageGen prompts, input images and
selected output hashes. `frame-master.png` is the four-panel blank frame;
the Empress, King of Swords, Maid of Cups, Cavalier of Swords and Fool
illustrations revise the costume families. The first World proof uses its
previously revised Chinese flying-figure component.

See `docs/design/tarot-faces-v1.md` and the Tarot proof viewer for the saved
production-sized compositions and checks.
