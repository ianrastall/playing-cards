# Design 2 structural study v2

This revision develops the selected Madder Lake structural study without changing
its ornament. The outer floral border now has corresponding double gold rules at
both edges: one against the ivory margin and one against the inner composition.

Four nested regions remain within one Madder Lake hue family:

| Region | Treatment | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outer floral border | Darkest and muted | Establishes the card's outer frame |
| Narrow inner border | Slightly lighter and clearer | Creates a measured transition inward |
| Broad vine field | Middle-lightness and softly muted | Gives the scrolling vines breathing room |
| Central toranj surround | Richest, deepest and most saturated | Pulls attention toward the teal center |

The order intentionally alternates emphasis rather than forming a simple dark-to-
light gradient. The field remains quiet while the center receives the strongest
red, making the teal medallion read as a jewel.

`../../sources/generated/design2-structural-study-v2/`. The side-by-side image is
`design2-structural-study-v2-comparison.jpg`.

This remains a composition and color study. Production reconstruction still
requires clean layered regions and exact geometric symmetry.
