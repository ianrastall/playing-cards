# Design 2 structural study v1

The current registered backs have sound individual motifs but weak visual
hierarchy and visible assembly artifacts. At the horizontal midpoint, the left
and right border rules and vines jump where the two rotated halves meet. The
inner edge also retains a separate dark vertical line. Because the field mask
was derived from the original dark artwork, colored variants retain dark matte
fringes around flowers and foliage.

The new Madder Lake study replaces that assembly with a calmer bookbinding
structure:

- one central toranj medallion as the primary focal point;
- smaller sar-toranj pendants above and below;
- four quarter-medallions anchored into the corners;
- a continuous, subordinate islimi vine instead of scattered filler;
- a single Madder Lake ground across border and field;
- concentric frames and border vines that turn through lotus corner motifs;
- fewer, broader shapes intended to remain legible at 63 x 88 mm.

The selected study and exact prompts are in
`../../sources/generated/design2-structural-study-v1/`. The comparison image is
`design2-structural-study-v1-comparison.jpg`.

This stage deliberately does not replace the production backs. Image generation
settles the artistic direction; a production pass must reconstruct the selected
art into clean layers, align its geometry, establish a clean field matte, and
then derive the remaining formats and colorways without recoloring fringes.
