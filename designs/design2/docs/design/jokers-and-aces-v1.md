# Design 2 Jokers and Aces

The two Jokers and four aces are generated with built-in ImageGen and saved
as 1060 × 1484 RGB masters with exact prompt records and SHA-256 manifests.

- [Two Jokers and prompts](../../sources/generated/jokers-v1/README.md)
- [Four aces and prompts](../../sources/generated/aces-v1/README.md)

The Jokers use Chinese-inspired entertainers with a folding fan and a small
pellet drum. The aces feature the existing lotus pip designs as large single
emblems, with the same red/black suit distinction and gold floral framing.

All six were reviewed for lettering, suit or prop readability, frame clearance
and design consistency. Source masters are preserved without subsequent image
processing. The prior courts and pip components were not replaced.

This completes the requested artwork pass for the Jokers and aces. The next
requested stage is the 36 number cards, ranks 2 through 10 in all four suits.
