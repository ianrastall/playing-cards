# Design 2 structural colorways v1

The selected toranj composition now has studies in all five Design 2 colors.
Each retains the double outer and inner gold frames and the four-step ground
hierarchy established by the Madder Lake study.

| Colorway | Broad field | Medallion interior | Foliage treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prussian Blue | Muted slate-Prussian | Deep green-teal | Warm olive |
| Verdigris | Muted mineral verdigris | Deep indigo with teal accents | Warm yellow-olive |
| Madder Lake | Softly muted madder | Deep green-teal | Warm olive |
| Manganese Violet | Dusty pigment violet | Deep green-teal | Warm olive |
| Lamp Black | Warm graphite | Deep green-teal | Warm olive |

The accent changes address the contrast problems in the earlier registered
backs. Verdigris no longer places teal medallions against a similar green field,
and its foliage is warmer than the ground. Prussian Blue keeps a clearer
blue-versus-green separation. Manganese Violet is softened to resemble pigment,
while Lamp Black uses value changes to reveal the four nested regions.

The four new full-resolution images and exact prompts are in
`../../sources/generated/design2-structural-colorways-v1/`. Madder Lake is in
`../../sources/generated/design2-structural-study-v2/`. The five-color review
sheet is `design2-structural-colorways-v1.jpg`.

These remain artistic studies. Production reconstruction must use shared clean
geometry and layered color regions rather than treating the generated files as
registered card backs.
